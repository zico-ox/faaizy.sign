💡 Hardware & Browser Requirements

1. Required Browser: Google Chrome on Android (v56+).

2. Protocol: Must be served via HTTPS or localhost (Web Bluetooth security requirement).

3. Thermal Printer Type: Portable or desktop ESC/POS thermal printer equipped with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

4. How to pair:

  • Turn on your thermal printer and verify Bluetooth is active (blue LED flashing or steady).
  • Enable Bluetooth and Location (required by Android for BLE scanning) on your Android phone.
  • Tap "Connect Printer" or "PRINT BILL". Select your printer in the Chrome device chooser popup.
